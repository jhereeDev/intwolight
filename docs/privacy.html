Privacy Policy

In Two Lights · last updated 3 August 2026

In Two Lights does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress — which puzzles you have solved and how precisely you solved them — using your device's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your payment details.

To recognise your purchase across reinstalls and devices, we use RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous identifier it generates for the installation — not linked to your name, email, or Apple/Google account;
  • the purchase receipt issued by the store;
  • basic device and app information, such as platform, operating system version, and app version.

We do not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, or any other identifying information, because the app never asks you for it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Network access

The game is fully playable offline. The only network requests it makes are to verify a purchase or restore a previous one.

Your choices

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete from our side. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. Purchase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat can be handled through those services directly.
